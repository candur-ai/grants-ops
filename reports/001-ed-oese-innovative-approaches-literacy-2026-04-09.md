### Blocking Issues

**1. No UEI (hard blocker)** — Cannot submit any federal grant application without a Unique Entity Identifier registered through SAM.gov.

**2. No EIN / 501(c)(3) determination (hard blocker)** — The NOFO requires nonprofits to document tax-exempt status via IRS recognition letter. AI Incubator for Main Street does not yet have an EIN or IRS determination.

**3. "Demonstrated history" requirement (hard blocker for nonprofits)** — Nonprofits applying to IAL must have "a demonstrated history of effectively developing and implementing literacy activities." As a new organization with no program history, this requirement cannot be met.

### Critical Mismatch: Definition of "Literacy"

**This is the most important finding.** The IAL program defines "literacy" as **reading and writing literacy** — specifically:
- School library programs and book access
- Pediatric literacy (doctors distributing books during well-child visits)
- High-quality book distribution to low-income children
- Reading intervention (Response-to-Intervention frameworks)

Springer's "literacy" focus is **financial literacy, AI literacy, and business literacy** — entirely different domains. The IAL program does not fund financial education, digital skills, AI training, or entrepreneurship programs. Framing Springer's curriculum as "innovative literacy" would be a fundamental mismatch with the program's statutory purpose under ESEA Title II.

## Recommendation: Don't Apply

**Score: 0.0/5 — SKIP**

Three independent hard blockers prevent application:
1. No UEI
2. No EIN / 501(c)(3) determination letter
3. No demonstrated history in (reading/writing) literacy programs

Even if all three blockers were resolved, the **core mission mismatch** makes this a poor fit. IAL funds reading books, school libraries, and early childhood reading programs — not the practical business skills, financial literacy, and AI education that Springer delivers.

### What This Means for Future Scans

The word "literacy" in federal grants almost always means **reading/writing literacy** unless explicitly qualified (e.g., "financial literacy," "digital literacy," "health literacy"). Future scans should not flag IAL-type programs as matches for Springer.
