## Your Mission Narrative

<!-- [CUSTOMIZE] One paragraph (~100 words) used as the opening framing in all
     project narratives. Should be compelling, specific, and jargon-free.
     Copy from profile.yml narrative section, then expand with persuasive detail. -->

AI Incubator for Main Street builds practical pathways for Kentucky's youth and small businesses to thrive in an AI-transformed economy. Our flagship program, **Springer**, is a camp that teaches teens the skills no one else is teaching: how to sell, how money works, how to network, how to use AI as a learning tool, and how to support themselves — whether through a side hustle, a service business, or a full-time venture. We don't teach kids to "build the next unicorn." We teach them to survive and thrive in the new world. School isn't preparing them for what's coming. We give them the tools to figure it out.

## Your Cross-cutting Advantage

<!-- [CUSTOMIZE] Your "signature move" — the 2-3 sentences that differentiate you

Every other youth entrepreneurship camp teaches pitch decks and business model canvases. Springer teaches kids how to sell something to another person, how banking and credit and taxes actually work, and how to use AI to learn anything they need to know. Our mentors aren't tech company execs — they're community leaders who run real local businesses. The result isn't a certificate; it's skills and a lasting support network. That's the gap no one else is filling.
